# Cinematic Look Workflow

## Technical base

Start with correct exposure, white balance, color management, and shot matching.

## Design the look

Define:

- tonal contrast
- palette
- saturation level
- highlight behavior
- shadow behavior
- color separation

## Apply consistently

Build the look from the story and lighting of the footage. Do not assume a fixed teal-orange recipe, LUT, or contrast curve is cinematic by itself.

## Review

Check the look across different shots, skin tones, lighting conditions, and the intended display.
