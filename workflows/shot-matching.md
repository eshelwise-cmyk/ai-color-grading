# Shot Matching

1. Choose a reference shot.
2. Normalize exposure and color management.
3. Match white balance.
4. Match contrast and tonal distribution.
5. Match saturation and major hue relationships.
6. Check skin tones when relevant.
7. Compare at the same viewing conditions.
8. Apply the creative look after technical consistency is established.

Match what the scene requires rather than forcing every shot to have identical waveform values.
