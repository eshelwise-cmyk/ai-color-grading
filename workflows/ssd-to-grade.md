# SSD to Grade

## 1. Ingest

Copy camera media to a working drive and create a verified backup. Do not grade directly from the only copy.

## 2. Organize

Group clips by camera, scene, take, and recording format. Preserve original filenames and metadata.

## 3. Identify source

Determine camera model, recording profile, gamma/log encoding, gamut, RAW settings, and frame rate where available.

## 4. Color management

Choose a consistent color-managed workflow. Convert the source into the working/display pipeline without confusing technical transforms with creative LUTs.

## 5. Technical correction

Balance exposure, white balance, contrast, and color neutrality.

## 6. Match

Match shots within the same scene before applying a broad creative look.

## 7. Creative grade

Shape palette, contrast, saturation, highlights, shadows, and skin-tone relationships.

## 8. QC

Check clipping, noise, artifacts, shot continuity, skin tones, and viewing conditions.

## 9. Export

Export to the required delivery color space and verify the rendered file.
